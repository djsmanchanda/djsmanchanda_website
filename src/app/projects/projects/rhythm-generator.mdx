## Overview

Rhythm Generator is a lightweight browser experiment for making interesting beats without a traditional sequencer. It keeps the controls approachable while exposing the moving parts underneath: timing, pattern variation, and the small decisions that make a loop feel alive.

## What It Explores

- **Pattern generation**: Create rhythmic variations from a compact set of user-adjustable controls.
- **Immediate feedback**: Keep playback and changes close to the interface so the result is easy to hear and iterate on.
- **Small, focused product design**: Make a playful tool feel understandable instead of burying the interaction under a large studio-style UI.

## Why It Belongs Here

This is the kind of project I use to stay close to the browser as a creative medium. It is deliberately smaller than the data-heavy systems in the rest of this portfolio, but the same bias applies: start with a clear interaction, make the behavior dependable, and leave room for another useful idea.
